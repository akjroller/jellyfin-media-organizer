# Acknowledgments

Jellyfin Media Organizer (JMO) began as a fork of [`mnamer`](https://github.com/jkwill87/mnamer), created and maintained by Jessy Williams (`@jkwill87`).

That project provided the original media-organizing groundwork and was released under the MIT License. JMO has since diverged into a separate Jellyfin-focused, plan-first design, but the upstream project's work remains an important part of this project's history.

The original MIT copyright and permission notice is retained in `LICENSE.txt` as required by the license.

JMO is independently developed and maintained. The upstream `mnamer` project and its maintainers are not responsible for JMO's current behavior, design decisions, or support.
